# Design Decisions Registry

Every design question that affects production, and its state. **Open items
must not be decided in code.** Implementation that depends on an open item is
blocked (`[!]`) until the developer records a decision here.

- **OPEN**: undecided; do not invent an answer.
- **DECIDED**: locked by the developer. The source is noted.
- **PROPOSED**: a production recommendation from the audit, awaiting approval.

| D-19 | **The player's items have one owner: the `Inventory` autoload (was O-14).** It holds the one `ItemStore` (never handed out) and is saved as `items`. | D-20 | **Coins live in a `Wallet` autoload, separate from Wriksha Points.** Points stay the score (`PointsManager`); coins are the future buying/selling currency (D-10). Only `credit`/`debit` change it. | D-22 | **Reward amounts are data; never-respawning discoveries are once-ever.** Flat Wriksha Points rewards are `RewardRule`s in `data/rewards/` (points only; per-definition amounts stay on discoveries/crops; the harvest quality scale stays FarmManager's rule, D-11). Repeat collection of respawning discoveries stays unlimited (no caps/diminishing returns — O-01 open; *D-25 closed O-01 without caps by keeping coins off every such source*). | D-23 | **Economy configuration is data read by no gameplay code.** `EconomyConfig` (`data/economy/economy_config.tres`) holds D-10's redemption reference exactly — 1000 coins = 10 INR (`redemption_reference_coins`, `_amount`, `_currency`) — for a future backend/redemption phase; nothing in scripts, scenes or UI reads or shows it, and it is not wired to the Wallet or to points. | D-24 | **Wriksha Points and coins are permanently independent (closes O-02).** Points stay the progression/achievement score (`PointsManager`), earned by all the existing reward sources, saved and shown exactly as before; coins stay the Wallet's future gameplay currency with no current sources or sinks (balance 0 until a later milestone adds legitimate coin rewards — *that milestone is M06.2: selling produce, D-25*). Points are never converted to or from coins and never mirrored into them; no reward pays both; no rate exists (`EconomyConfig`'s 1000 coins = ₹10 is a redemption reference only, D-23). Of O-02's documented options this is "keep both" | Developer instruction (M05.5) |
| D-25 | **The coin economy: coins are earned only by selling produce (closes O-01).** Harvest → produce enters the Inventory → the player sells it (Basket screen, confirm first) → the Wallet receives coins. **Sources:** only `Market.sell(item_id, quality, quantity) -> int` — the `Market` autoload is the one caller of `Wallet.credit()`. Harvest itself pays **no** coins (points and produce exactly as before); discoveries (first or repeat), the daily discovery, landmarks, secrets, curiosity, discovery thresholds, farm milestones, collectibles, seeds and Wriksha Points pay **no** coins. **Sellable:** only items with `category == "produce"` and `sell_value >= 1` (`ItemDefinition.sell_value`, 0 = unsellable); seeds and collectibles never sell. **Prices (locked, independent of `points_value`):** base `sell_value` wild_carrot 5, meadow_herb 6, golden_sunflower 8, elderbloom 12 (the Good price); quality percents (`SellRules`, `data/market/sell_rules.tres`) Plain 80 / Good 100 / Fine 140 — a coin rule of its own, never `QUALITY_POINT_SCALE`; `unit = (sell_value × percent + 50) div 100` (whole numbers, half up, per unit), `total = unit × quantity` — so carrot 4/5/7, herb 5/6/8, sunflower 6/8/11, elderbloom 10/12/17, and 3 Fine carrots = 21. **Sale:** validate (item, produce, sell_value, quality, quantity ≥ 1, held) → price (≥ 1) → remove the items → credit once → if the credit is refused, put the exact items back → `produce_sold`; **one ledger entry per confirmed sale**, reason exactly `sell:<item_id>:<quality>` (no price, text or time). GameState saves on `produce_sold`, so items and wallet are saved together; the Market never saves. **No sinks** in M06.2 (no `debit` caller: no seeds, plots, services, cosmetics, trade, gifts or redemption for coins). No caps or cap state (the only coin faucet is produce that exists). **No retroactive coins** for existing saves; produce already held sells normally. `SAVE_VERSION` stays 5 (no new saved state). Points and coins stay independent (D-24); EconomyConfig stays unread and the D-10 reference outside all gameplay and UI (D-23) — the coin balance is its own "Coins N", never ✿ points, never ₹ *(M08.7, D-40: amended — a second coin source, NPC trades of collectibles through `Market.trade()`; the Market stays the only `Wallet.credit()` caller and the Basket still sells only produce; still no sinks)* | Developer decisions after the M06.1 audit (O-01 A–J; values approved M06.2) |
| D-26 | **Mobile UI language and HUD layout (M06.3).** The UI is designed on the 1080 × 1920 portrait canvas with one shared theme, `scenes/ui/wriksha_theme.tres`, in the existing parchment / bark / leaf palette. Interactive controls on the HUD, Basket and Inventory are **at least 120 canvas px**. The HUD's top bar is **anchored to the top and sized by its content** (never stretched): ✿ points pill, discovery count, then 📚 📖 ⭐ 🕹; notifications below it; **🧺 Basket and 🎒 Inventory bottom-right** in the thumb zone (🧺 still shown only while produce is held); both follow the display's safe area. **Points and coins stay visually separate** — the ✿ points pill vs the amber "Coins N" chip, never one label (D-24). Cards keep one system: the harvest card keeps the care note ("watered with care" / "left thirsty") so the care rule stays learnable; the sale card shows CROP / quality ×qty / +N Coins from the Market's reported values. The Inventory stays read-only. Collection, Journal, Daily and the SeedPicker are deliberately unchanged in M06.3 (a later UI pass). Presentation only: no gameplay, economy or save change | Developer decisions (M06.3 audit: numbering, care note kept, other modals deferred, button split, static checks only) |
| D-27 | **Farm milestones go through one reward path, keyed by milestone id (M06.4).** `FarmManager._reach(id, message)` is the only way a farm milestone happens: it records the id once ever (saved `farm.milestones`), pays the reward the reward data names for that id (`data/rewards/<id>.tres`; no rule = no reward — callers never choose an amount), opens the plots waiting on it, then announces `milestone_reached` (GameState saves, the HUD shows the card). The **milestone id is the progression hook**: plots' `unlock_on_milestone`, `MilestoneReveal.milestone_id`, reward rules and the Journal (`get_milestones()`, the registry) all name registered ids, checked statically. Milestones are reached only from game events (planting, ripening, harvesting), never from UI. Amounts unchanged (first harvest 10, all starter crops 40, starter garden 30, garden in bloom 50; others 0); points only (D-24); no save change. Exploration bonuses keep their own path until M13.1; the overall progression structure stays open (O-04) | Developer instruction (M06.4, from the plan's "one reward path") |
| D-28 | **The pond has a controlled walkable edge (closes O-07, option B; M07.3).** The pond keeps its look and position; inside its 3.6 m water a blocked core of radius 2.8 m (a `StaticBody3D` cylinder plus a matching `NavigationObstacle3D` that carves the baked navigation mesh) stops the joystick and tap-to-move, leaving a walkable shallow edge (≈ 0.45 m of water past the carve, then the bank). Chosen from the runtime audit: the pond was fully walkable by accident (the player stood on the water), while a pond solid at its full water radius left the two discoveries placed in the water (Wild Mint, River Stone) out of interaction reach. With the core, both are collected from the edge; tapping the core does nothing. No wading/swimming visuals (later art). Discoveries, the pond and the path are not moved | Developer decision (M07.3, after the runtime audit) |
| D-29 | **The follow camera never pulls in on geometry (M07.3).** The `SpringArm3D` in `FollowCamera.tscn` ignores collisions (`collision_mask = 0`), so the camera stays at its arm length everywhere. | D-30 | **Wrikshagandha is a mobile-first landscape game (locked product decision; M07.4).** Landscape / horizontal, composed for 16:9 (wider phones get more width, never a different layout), Android phones first; desktop is for development and testing only. D-26's UI language stays: its sizes are canvas pixels on the 1080-px short side, so touch targets (≥ 120 px), text and buttons keep their physical size; its "1080 × 1920 portrait canvas" is superseded. | D-31 | **The outdoor world, farming included, pauses while the player is indoors (closes O-11; M08.1).** While the Meadow is parked (D-33) nothing in it advances: time of day, discovery respawn timers, environmental events, wildlife — and the farm: a plot's growth timer stands still and its thirst does not count, exactly the rule for time away from the app. Mechanism: leaving captures every plot (`FarmManager.release_plots_in`, M03.3); returning re-registers the same plot nodes (`FarmManager.register_plot`), which restores the captured state and resumes thirst from its captured duration. A save made indoors holds the captured plots. No farm rule, value or save field changes (D-11 stays frozen; `FarmPlot.restore()` only learned to replace a crop visual it already shows). | Developer, M08 approval |
| D-32 | **Interior camera bounds limit the camera's focus, not its view (closes O-12; M08.1/M08.2).** As in the Meadow (M03.4), an area's `AreaCameraBounds` clamps the point the camera follows; nothing keeps the whole view inside. | D-33 | **The Meadow stays alive while the player is inside (M08.1).** Leaving an area whose data says `keep_alive_when_left` (the Meadow) parks it: Main removes it from the tree and keeps the same instance; returning puts that instance back. Nothing in it resets or re-runs — so the day does not jump back to its start and collected discoveries do not respawn on a round trip (a free-and-reload swap does both: a points exploit). Other areas (the home interior) are freed when left and built fresh. | Developer, M08 approval |
| D-34 | **Areas are data; AreaRouter asks, Main carries out; the current area is not saved (M08.1).** One `AreaDefinition` per `data/areas/*.tres` (id, display name, scene, keep-alive, camera distance); no area, entry or area scene is named in code. | D-35 | **Doors use the existing interaction flow; walking into a door never travels (M08.1).** A door is an `AreaDoor` Interactable (verbs ENTER or EXIT from D-09) reached like anything else — tapped, walked to, then used; its `interact()` only asks `AreaRouter.travel()`. It never reacts to the player walking into it, and Player and InputManager stay unaware of doors. | Developer, M08 approval |
| D-36 | **The NPC framework (M08.3).** NPCs are data: one `NpcDefinition` per `data/npcs/*.tres` (id, display name, greeting, wander radius); the `Npc` scene consumes its definition and stands where it is placed (the first, a neutral placeholder villager — explicitly not a Rishi, R-01–R-07 stay open — at the Meadow's `NpcSpot`). It idles and wanders within ~2 m on the area's own navigation map through its own NavigationAgent3D, keeps clear of door entries, holds still from the moment a tap chooses it and while the player is within 4 m (facing them), and is physically solid (a CharacterBody3D the player collides with; never in the static bake) — stepping aside when a player is about to walk into it or presses against it, so walks past it never deadlock. Talking is an ordinary Interactable (`NpcTalk`, verb **TALK = 10**, appended): tap → walk into range → `interact()` → the greeting in the HUD's `SpeechPanel` (bottom-centre, shared theme, safe area, 120 px ✕, non-modal; closes on ✕, on walking out of range, or when the Meadow is parked). **No autoload:** the conversation state is the panel's own (its speaker); NpcTalk reaches it through its group. | D-37 | **Minimal data dialogue (M08.4) — the plan's "conditions, outcomes" narrowed.** A conversation is data: one `DialogueDefinition` per `data/dialogues/*.tres` — exactly an `id` (its file name) and 2–8 ordered `lines` (≤ 120 characters each, never naming a Rishi) — with no branches, conditions, outcomes or logic. The panel stays non-modal, bottom-centre and safe-area docked. Unchanged: TALK = 10 and every verb, tap → walk → interact, the NPC holding still and facing while the player is in reach, Player/InputManager, furniture, saves (`SAVE_VERSION` 5); no relationships, affection, quests, requests, services or schedules. | Developer, M08.4 brief |

| D-39 | **Requests (M08.6).** One request per NPC at most, entirely data: `RequestDefinition` in `data/requests/` (id = file name, npc_id, item_id of a **single-quality** item, quantity 1–9, reward_points ≥ 1, and three ordinary `DialogueDefinition`s — offer, pending, hand-over).
